# 3 more Israel-first, AI-managed side projects

Same playbook as the scheduler: **phone/WhatsApp-native, thin scope, ~100 paying logos, you + AI run it** — no desktop, no marketplace day one.

Israel context: WhatsApp is the business OS ([Times of Israel](https://blogs.timesofisrael.com/how-whatsapp-runs-israeli-small-business/)); generic WA BSPs (Gambot etc.) exist at ~₪179+/mo but are broad. These three are **vertical wedges** with clearer jobs and stickier workflows.

---

## 1) ועד בית OS — “Vaad”

### Job
Building committees (ועדי בתים) run on **WhatsApp groups + Excel + Bit/PayBox chaos**: announcements, who paid ועד, contractor quotes, votes, water/elevator issues.

### Product (thin)
- One WA number / web mini-app per building  
- Resident directory + unit  
- Monthly dues tracker + payment link  
- Announcements + AI FAQ (“כמה אני חייב?”, “מתי בא הטכנאי?”)  
- Simple poll/vote  
- Vendor quote inbox (PDF → summary)

### Who pays
Vaad chair / management company — **per building**, not per resident.

### Pricing sketch
| Plan | Price | Includes |
|------|-------|----------|
| Building | **₪79/mo** | ≤40 units, dues + announce + AI FAQ |
| Large | **₪129/mo** | ≤120 units + polls + vendor inbox |

**100 buildings × ₪90 ARPU ≈ ₪9k MRR.**

### Why AI-managed works
Residents ask the same 15 questions; AI answers from building knowledge base. You rarely talk to residents — only vaad chairs.

### Compete / risk
- Low software competition that is WA-native + dues  
- Risk: slow sales (committees), unpaid vaads, need trust  
- Moat: data per building + habit  

### First 10 customers
Friends’ buildings, Facebook “ועד בית” groups, property managers with 5–20 buildings.

## 2) Trades desk — “Makor” (חשמל / אינסטלציה / שיפוץ / מיזוג)

### Job
Home-service pros live in WhatsApp: lead → site visit → **הצעת מחיר** → chase → deposit → schedule. Leads die after hours; quotes sit in chat history.

### Product (thin)
- AI WhatsApp receptionist (hours, service area, rough price band)  
- Lead card → “send quote” templates (HE)  
- Quote → approve → **deposit link** (PayPlus/Grow)  
- Job status: new / quoted / won / scheduled / done  
- Reminder to chase unpaid quotes (D+1, D+3)

**Not** a full Fieldwire / Jobber clone. Not generic Gambot.

### Who pays
Solo / 2-person trades — same ICP energy as Plannie solos.

### Pricing sketch
| Plan | Price | Includes |
|------|-------|----------|
| Solo | **₪99/mo** | 1 number, AI inbox, quotes, deposits, fair-use msgs |
| Crew | **₪149/mo** | 3 users, shared inbox |

**100 solos × ₪110 ARPU ≈ ₪11k MRR.**

### Why AI-managed works
80% of inbound is “זמין השבוע?”, “כמה עולה…?”, “שלח הצעה”. AI + templates; human only for complex quotes.

### Compete / risk
- Agencies charge ₪3.5k+ setup + ₪200–400/mo for generic WA API  
- You win on **vertical templates + quote→deposit**, not raw API  
- Risk: Meta policy, spammy trades, quote quality  

### First 10 customers
Facebook groups for שיפוצניקים, supplier shops (שפע, Ace), “ask your electrician” referrals.

## 3) Shift confirm — “Mishmar” (cafés / bars / small restaurants)

### Job
Hospitality schedules are WhatsApp lists: “מי מגיע בשישי?” No-shows and last-minute swaps kill managers. Full WFM (Restigo/Lynxbe) is heavy/expensive for a 8–25 person café.

### Product (thin)
- Manager builds week on phone  
- Each shift → WA confirm / decline button  
- Open-shift broadcast to eligible staff  
- Late / no-confirm alerts to manager  
- Simple timesheet export (CSV for accountant)

**Not** full payroll, tip pooling, or inventory.

### Who pays
Café / bar / small restaurant owner or shift manager.

### Pricing sketch
| Plan | Price | Includes |
|------|-------|----------|
| Floor | **₪89/mo** | ≤15 staff |
| Busy | **₪129/mo** | ≤40 staff + open-shift broadcast |

**100 venues × ₪100 ARPU ≈ ₪10k MRR.**

### Why AI-managed works
Support is “how do I publish next week?” — docs + AI. Staff never email you; they tap WA buttons.

### Compete / risk
- High churn industry (venues die)  
- Win on **simplicity + WA**, lose if you chase enterprise WFM  
- Labor-law messaging: stay a confirm tool, not a legal HR system  

### First 10 customers
Your local coffee strip, F&B Facebook groups, one soft-branded “for independents” landing page.

## Side-by-side

| | Vaad (buildings) | Makor (trades) | Mishmar (shifts) |
|--|------------------|----------------|------------------|
| ARPU | ₪80–130 | ₪99–149 | ₪89–129 |
| 100-logo MRR | ~₪9k | ~₪11k | ~₪10k |
| Sales cycle | Slow (committee) | Fast (solo) | Medium |
| Churn | Low | Medium | High |
| AI leverage | Very high (FAQ) | High (inbox+quotes) | Medium (flows) |
| Regret risk | Trust/payments | Meta + quality | Venue death |
| Best if you like… | Local community ops | SMB sales hustle | Hospitality |

---

## Pick order (opinionated)

1. **Makor (trades)** — fastest to cash, same muscle as scheduler (solo IL SMB + WA + deposits).  
2. **Vaad** — stickiest, most “only in Israel,” slower sales.  
3. **Mishmar** — clear wedge vs heavy WFM; watch churn.

All three stay **AI-operable at ~100 clients** with the same 30h/week pattern: build thin → WA-first → Hebrew UX → you sell, AI supports.

---

## What not to build (crowded / heavy)

- Another Green Invoice / Morning clone  
- Generic WhatsApp BSP (Gambot lane)  
- Full clinic EMR  
- Marketplace “find a professional”  
- Desktop-first tools
