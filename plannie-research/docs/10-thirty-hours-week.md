# If you invest 30 hours/week — what you can cover

Baseline from [09-side-hustle-plan](09-side-hustle-plan.md): thin phone-first product, AI support, ~100 clients.  
**30h/week** is no longer “nights and weekends only” — it’s a serious part-time founder allocation (~0.75 FTE).

---

## 1. How to split the 30h (recommended)

| Bucket | Hours/week | Purpose |
|--------|------------|---------|
| **Build** | 16–18 | Ship MVP → harden → 1–2 wedge features |
| **Growth** | 6–8 | Outbound, content, onboarding calls, partnerships |
| **Ops** | 4–6 | Support escalations, billing, reliability, AI tuning |
| **Buffer** | 2 | Incidents, accounting, App Store if needed |

Keep AI on L1 support so Ops doesn’t eat Build.

---

## 2. Product coverage (what 30h can actually ship)

### Phase A — weeks 1–8 (~first 120–140h build)

**Fully covered (MVP you can charge for):**
- Phone OTP auth  
- Business + services + profession templates  
- Weekly hours → auto slots (no windows)  
- Public booking link (mobile)  
- Pro day calendar + appointment sheet  
- WA utility reminders + SMS fallback  
- Basic clients list + import  
- Starter/Pro billing (PayPlus/PayPal)  
- Hebrew + English UI  
- AI help center / ticket draft replies  

**Not covered yet:** punch cards, campaigns, multi-staff, deposits KYC depth, insights, native stores.

### Phase B — weeks 9–16 (with growth starting)

**Add with remaining build capacity:**
- Deposits (IL PSP)  
- Waiting-list + approve queue polish  
- Multi-staff (2–4) for Studio tier  
- Template studio (edit reminder copy)  
- Simple rebook / follow-up automation  
- Admin console for you (impersonate, credits, churn flags)

**Still skip:** desktop, marketplace, POS, gift cards, public API, heavy analytics.

### What 30h does *not* buy
- Feature parity with Plannie Premium  
- Marketplace / lead-gen  
- Agency-style white-glove for every client  
- Two platforms (iOS+Android native) *and* fast iteration — prefer **PWA** until 50+ paid

## 3. Client / revenue coverage

| Horizon | Paying clients | MRR @ ₪59 ARPU | Your role |
|---------|----------------|----------------|-----------|
| Month 2–3 | 10–25 | ₪0.6–1.5k | Hands-on onboard every logo |
| Month 4–6 | 40–70 | ₪2.4–4.1k | Mix self-serve + you close |
| Month 6–9 | **100–150** | ₪5.9–9k | AI + playbooks; you do sales spikes |
| Month 9–12 | **150–250** | ₪9–15k | Possible if churn &lt;5% and WA-first |

**Vs 8–12h/week:** 30h mainly accelerates **time-to-MVP and acquisition**, not max theoretical clients. Ops ceiling with AI is still ~150–250 before you need help or harder automation.

**Comfortable target with 30h:** **150 paying** by month 9–12, not just 100 — if you spend the growth hours.

---

## 4. Growth coverage (the 6–8h/week)

With consistent growth time you can cover:

| Channel | What you do |
|---------|-------------|
| Direct | 10–20 WhatsApp/IG DMs to salons/barbers per week |
| Groups | FB/WA beauty & small-biz communities (IL) |
| Referral | In-product “give 1 month / get 1 month” |
| Content | 1 short Hebrew Reel/post weekly (AI drafts, you film) |
| Partners | 2–3 accountant / chair-supplier / course-teacher intros |
| Light ads | Only after conversion works; cap CAC ₪250 |

**Expected acquisition rate with 30h:** ~8–15 new trials/mo → ~5–10 paid/mo once MVP is live.  
That’s enough to **reach 100 in ~6–9 months** from launch (not from today if product isn’t built).

---

## 5. Ops coverage at scale

| Clients | Ops hours/week (AI on) | Fits in 4–6h Ops bucket? |
|---------|------------------------|---------------------------|
| 50 | 2–4 | Yes |
| 100 | 4–7 | Tight — automate more |
| 150 | 6–10 | **Steals from Build** unless playbooks rock |
| 250 | 10–15 | Hire part-time CS or cut scope |

**Rule:** if Ops &gt; 8h/week, freeze features and kill support causes (onboarding bugs, SMS failures, billing confusion).

---

## 6. Economic upside of 30h vs 10h

| | ~10h/week | **30h/week** |
|--|-----------|--------------|
| MVP live | Slow (4–6 mo) | **8–10 weeks** realistic |
| 100 clients | 12–18 mo | **6–9 mo** post-launch |
| Steady MRR | ₪5–7k | ₪6–12k (push to 150–200) |
| Your “wage” if ₪5k profit / 30h×4 | — | ~₪40/h cash — OK learning biz; better as equity in a product you own |
| Burnout risk | Low | Medium — protect Build hours |

30h doesn’t 3× revenue automatically; it **3× speed to a chargeable product + distribution**.

---

## 8. Suggested 12-week sprint (30h/week)

| Weeks | Focus | Exit |
|-------|-------|------|
| 1–4 | Core book + hours + calendar | Internal dogfood |
| 5–8 | WA reminders + billing + HE | First 5–10 paid |
| 9–12 | Deposits + onboarding polish + growth loop | 25–40 paid, clear CAC |

Then decide: hold at hustle (~100–150) or go harder (hire / raise / quit day job).

---

## Bottom line

**30h/week covers a real MVP and a path past 100 clients** — roughly **100–150 paying** with ~₪6–9k MRR in under a year post-launch — if you keep scope thin and spend ~25% of time on growth.  

It does **not** cover out-building Plannie or running 250+ logos without either more automation or a second human.
